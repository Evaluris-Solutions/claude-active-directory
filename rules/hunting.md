# Engagement rules — Active Directory (always active)

**Claude Active Directory** — [Evaluris Solutions](https://evaluris.ae). Authorized testing only.

1. **ROE first** — No testing without written scope: domains, forests, DCs, subnets, accounts, destructive vs read-only.
2. **Exclusions are absolute** — If a host or technique is out of scope, do not touch it.
3. **Evidence** — Every reported finding needs reproducible steps and redacted proof.
4. **Kill weak leads** — False positives waste client time and trust; drop early.
5. **No unauthorized access** — Criminal law applies; this toolkit is for legal engagements only.
6. **Lockout awareness** — Password attacks only with explicit approval and policy review.
7. **Production caution** — Assume monitoring; prefer lab or customer-approved windows for noisy activity.
8. **Chain honesty** — Document multi-hop paths end-to-end; do not overstate single-step issues.
9. **Segregation** — Customer data and credentials do not belong in public repos or chat logs.
10. **Validate before deliverables** — Use `/validate` and internal QA for client reports.
