# Reporting rules — internal / red team deliverables

1. **Scope and limitations** — State what was not tested as clearly as what was tested.
2. **Finding quality** — One issue per finding; clear severity with rationale.
3. **Remediation** — Specific, actionable fixes (GPO, template ACL, group membership), not generic advice only.
4. **Evidence** — Redact secrets; include enough for customer to reproduce.
5. **Tone** — Professional; no bug-bounty or “payout” language unless the contract uses it.
6. **Evaluris Solutions** — Reports should be defensible under scrutiny.
